ARXIV CS.CL
Computation and Language

Recent research addresses critical challenges in conversational AI, ranging from security vulnerabilities and model compression for structured generation to long-term memory maintenance and efficient retrieval. These studies collectively advance the reliability, efficiency, and safety of large language model agents across diverse multilingual and multi-turn environments.

1
Interactive Backdoor Attacks in LLM-Based Proactive Dialogue Systems
The authors present an interactive backdoor attack framework for LLM-based proactive dialogue systems using training-data poisoning to steer multi-turn interactions toward attacker-specified objectives while preserving normal utility.
Zhiyang Xia, Yuming Shang and others · cs.CL, cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
2
SKIM: Pruning Large Language Model Agents via Selective Knowledge Informed Masking
The authors propose SKIM, a token-role-guided pruning and distillation framework for LLM agents under structured generation, which improves accuracy over baselines particularly in high-sparsity regimes.
Moonseok Choi, Jongwon Jeong and others · cs.CL, cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
3
MedGraph: Maintaining Patient State with Graph Memory for Long-Term Medical Dialogue
The authors introduce MedGraph, a graph memory system for longitudinal medical dialogue that extracts dated facts and links clinical records into chains, achieving the highest overall accuracy on MedMemoryBench.
Donghua Zhang, Zizheng Wang and others · cs.CL, cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
4
Persistent Retrieval for Conversational Agents: A Study of Retrieval Efficiency
The authors compare conversational agent retrieval designs and find that a persistent retrieval subagent reduces API costs and token usage compared to skill-based retrieval while improving response times.
Karthik Ravula, Sathwik Kowtha and others · cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.IR  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
5
{\tau}-bench-LILT: Evaluating Conversational Agents on Realistic Multilingual Tool-Use Tasks
The authors present tau-bench-LILT, a multilingual extension of tau3-bench covering four languages and domains, and find significant performance drops for agent models in specific non-English retail settings.
Kaden Uhlig, Kiarash Mokhtari and others · cs.CL  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
 
ARXIV CS.LG
Machine Learning

Recent research addresses the rapid expansion of the Internet of Things by examining energy-aware Tiny Machine Learning on resource-constrained platforms. The studies highlight heterogeneous methodologies and optimization strategies while emphasizing the need for greater standardization to improve reproducibility.

1
Energy Measurement and Optimization of TinyML in Resource-Constrained Embedded Systems: A Systematic Mapping Study
A systematic mapping study of energy-aware TinyML research identifies that current literature relies heavily on convolutional architectures and direct measurements, while revealing a lack of standardization in experimental platforms and reporting practices that hinders reproducibility.
Clariele Almeida, Renan Beserra and others · cs.LG, cs.AR, cs.ET  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
