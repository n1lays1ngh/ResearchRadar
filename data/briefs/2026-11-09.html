Recent research addresses critical security, architectural, ethical, and practical challenges in conversational AI and language model deployment. MemoryDojo: Benchmarking Poisoning Through LLM Agent Memory
The authors introduce MemoryDojo, a benchmark for evaluating poisoning across five agent memory systems, and find that all tested architectures are susceptible to malicious instructions propagated through multi-session tasks.

Compaction, decay and reinforcement in a two-tier conversational memory: a paired evaluation against retrieval-augmented generation
The authors compare conversational memory compaction against retrieval-augmented generation on LoCoMo and report that compaction achieves higher accuracy on value-based answers while utilizing fewer prompt tokens.
Osamudiamen Edogun, Joshua Obigwe and others · cs.CL  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

ABHBench: Evaluating Moral Decision-Making of Foundation Models from an Agentic Perspective
The authors introduce ABHBench, a multimodal benchmark based on an interactive narrative game, and find that 75 percent of evaluated foundation models shift their moral behavior significantly when placed in situated agentic contexts.
Chaoran Li, Yukun Li and others · cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CY  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

On Seeding Watermarks to Detect Verbatim LLM Copy-Paste Responses
The authors propose SteganoPrompt, an input-side watermarking tool that embeds invisible instructions into assignment prompts using the Unicode Tags block to detect verbatim copy-pasted LLM responses.
Aizierjiang Aiersilan, Artin Yousefi and others · cs.CR, cs.AI, cs.CL · cross-listed  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
5
Same Question, Different Answers: Auditing Persona-Dependent Variation in Six LLMs on Smoking-Cessation Advice
The authors audit six large language models on smoking-cessation advice across twelve user personas and find that the generated medical advice varies substantially depending on the persona asking the question.
Artin Yousefi, Aizierjiang Aiersilan and others · cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG · cross-listed  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
