Peer Memory Changes Who LLM Agents Consult Without a Detected Gain in Answer Accuracy

Jointly Optimizing Memory, Computing and Offloading for Energy-Efficient DNN Inference
The authors propose a joint optimization scheme integrating memory, computing, and offloading for energy-efficient deep neural network inference, demonstrating through simulation that the algorithm achieves near-optimal performance with a maximum gap of 9.3% and reduces device energy consumption by up to 16.1% compared to benchmarks.

New research addresses the degradation-aware participation of battery energy storage systems in frequency regulation markets. The findings demonstrate that strategic capacity bidding and deliberate performance score trade-offs can significantly extend battery life with minimal revenue impact.

Degradation-Aware Participation of Battery Storage in Frequency Regulation Markets
Researchers decompose the degradation-aware frequency regulation problem into bidding and operations layers, finding that adaptive bidding and sacrificing performance scores to avoid extreme states of charge can lengthen battery life by up to 31 percent with negligible revenue loss.
