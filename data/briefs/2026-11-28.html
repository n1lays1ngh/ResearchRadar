Computation and Language

Recent research addresses core challenges in conversational AI, dialogue systems, and large language model agents. The studies introduce novel benchmarks and frameworks to improve multi-agent routing reliability, memory utility, dataset construction, and linguistic adaptability.

1
CypherMAS: Benchmarking Routing Reliability in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems
The authors introduce CypherMAS, a synthetic benchmark isolating communication and routing in fixed multi-agent graphs, finding that routing reliability serves as a distinct scaling bottleneck for large language model systems.

Leto: Utility-Centric Agent Memory
The authors present Leto, a utility-centric memory system for large language model agents that frames memory as a prediction problem to anticipate future context needs, outperforming existing systems on personalization benchmarks such as PrefEval and PERMA.
Shuguang Chen and Salman Paracha · cs.CL  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

MGMem: An Efficient, Deterministic, and Provenance-Preserving Framework for Long-Horizon Agent Memory
The authors propose MGMem, a framework replacing generative indexing with a deterministic discriminative-parser pipeline, and report that it outperforms matched-protocol baselines on multi-session dialogue question answering.

Evaluating Large Language Models on conversational African American English: Feature classification and next-turn generation
The authors evaluate large language models on authentic conversational African American English, discovering that while a reasoning-enabled model excels at feature detection, all tested models underproduce the variety and generate uniform continuations.

ARXIV CS.LG
Machine Learning

Recent research in energy-efficient machine learning explores resource-optimized frameworks across agricultural forecasting, large language model inference, and genome sequencing. These studies demonstrate significant reductions in computational overhead, energy consumption, and hardware latency through innovative algorithmic and architectural designs.

1
Resource-Efficient Multimodal Fusion for Early Rice Blast Forecasting in Sustainable Agriculture
The authors propose a lightweight multimodal fusion framework combining a Transformer and a CNN-embedded gated recurrent unit to forecast rice blast risk under computational and energy constraints, achieving a validation accuracy of 96.93.
Shubhajyoti Das, Pritam Bikram and others · cs.LG  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
2
Pando: Noise-Coupled Parallel Draft Trees for Efficient Edge Speculative Decoding
The authors introduce Pando, a dynamic draft tree method for speculative decoding on edge devices that removes draft chain length caps via noise-coupled pruning, attaining a 5.3 times speedup and 4.2 times energy reduction over autoregressive decoding on a representative edge device.
Joren Dumoulin, Hyoukjun Kwon and others · cs.LG  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
3
Motivo: A Motif-Based Algorithm-Architecture Co-Design for Fast and Energy-Efficient Graph-Based Genome Analysis
Read mapping, a key step of genome sequence analysis, matches each fragment of a sequenced genome (a read) to its location in a reference genome.
Eirini Maria Tzermpou, Konstantina Koliogeorgi and others · cs.AR, cs.DC, q-bio.GN · cross-listed  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
4
Improving Building Energy Efficiency with an Indoor Environmental World Model and Counterfactual Adaptive Learning
The authors develop an occupant-centric indoor climate control framework featuring environmental world models and counterfactual adaptive learning, which reduces energy consumption by 11.2%, occupant discomfort by 44.2%, and action fluctuation by 47.3% compared to baseline methods.
Xu Yang, Kailai Sun and others · cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
5
Is 4 Bits Enough for EEG Sleep Staging? A Subject-Level Benchmark of MXFP4, NVFP4 and FP8
The authors benchmark post-training quantization configurations down to block-scaled 4-bit formats for EEG sleep staging, finding that keeping the first and last layers in FP8 brings 4-bit formats within 0.48 percentage points of FP32 Macro-F1 without requiring retraining.

ARXIV EESS.SY
Systems and Control

Recent research introduces non-invasive magnetic field mapping to detect internal leakage currents in lithium-ion cells and establishes a stochastic mathematical model for lunar power harvesting and battery recharging under random pointing errors. These studies advance battery diagnostics and storage analysis in complex operating environments.

1
Diagnosing internal leakage currents through magnetometry of lithium-ion cells
The authors report a non-invasive magnetometry method using a magnetoresistive sensor and linear stages to map magnetic fields above lithium-ion pouch cells, finding that mechanically damaged cells develop distinct zero-current magnetic fields and symmetry-breaking charge-discharge patterns that reveal internal leakage currents down to approximately 1 to 3 mA at rest.
Shaurya Shukla, Chengtong Zhang and others · physics.app-ph, physics.ins-det  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
2
Modeling Lunar Power Harvesting and Battery Recharging In The Presence of Random Pointing Error
Researchers established a lunar power beaming framework to derive probability density functions for state-of-charge, accumulated energy, and efficiency under random pointing errors, finding that slow-charging efficiency remains immune to such errors while utilizing a fraction of the link's potential capacity.
Bar{\i}\c{s} D\"onmez, Yanni Jiwan-Mercier and others · eess.SP  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
