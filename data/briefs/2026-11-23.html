Recent research addresses evaluation challenges in conversational AI and large language model systems, introducing benchmarks and methodologies to measure role integrity, statistical physics properties, cross-episode self-improvement, attribution protocols, and precedent-induced policy violations in autonomous agents.

Do Senior Agents Do Their Job? Measuring Role Integrity in How LLM Agents Use Subordinate Material
The authors introduce RI-Bench to measure role integrity in multi-agent systems and find that senior LLM agents rarely adopt procedural errors but frequently follow majority generic reviews or drop witness limitations.
Jinxiang Xie, Xinyi Zhang and others · cs.CL, cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

Potential emergence in LLM agents: a statistical physics perspective
The authors propose agent states as intermediate variables and utilize a statistical physics perspective to study the emergence of a global potential governing state transitions in LLM agent reasoning during post-training.
Zhuo-Yang Song and Hua Xing Zhu · cs.LG, cond-mat.stat-mech, cs.AI  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

AstronautBench: Measuring Agent Self-Improvement Across Episodes Through Knowledge, Evidence, and Progress
The authors introduce AstronautBench, which uses three views on Outer Wilds gameplay to measure agent self-improvement, finding that these metrics do not move together across episodes.
Congyuan Yu and Runping Tian · cs.AI, cs.CL  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
4
Separating Automation Gains from Agent Contributions in AI-Assisted Model Validation
The authors propose a staged architecture and attribution protocol to separate automation gains from agent contributions in model validation, demonstrating that naive evaluations can produce misleadingly tied scores.
M. Ahmadi Achachlouei · cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.SE  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
5
Correct Precedents Induce Policy Violations in On-Device LLM Agents
The authors identify correct-precedent generalization where on-device LLM agents are led into policy violations by prior correct decisions and propose Paired Decision Distillation to restore safe rates.
Chong Tian, Yu Wang and others · cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CR  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss

Efficient Natural Language to Temporal Logic Translation for Real-Time Physical AI
The authors report a System-One classification model that translates natural language into linear temporal logic by answering pre-defined questions, achieving accuracy comparable to or exceeding local large language models at up to one order of magnitude lower runtime and energy consumption.
