Computation and Language

Recent research in conversational artificial intelligence and language model systems examines biomedical agent memory frameworks, social media simulation pipelines, cognitive graph retrieval architectures, and personality-driven cultural dialogue adaptation. Additional studies investigate shifting human evaluation standards in chatbot benchmarking over time.

1
BioMem: Agentic Memory for Biomedical LLM Agents
The authors investigate agentic memory across biomedical large language models and introduce Hybrid Memory Retrieval and Utilization, a training-free framework that achieves an average relative improvement of 8.6% across biomedical question-answering and conversational memory benchmarks.
Mingchen Li, Yongkang Xiao and others · cs.CL  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
2
Using LLM Agents to Evaluate Prosocial Interventions on Social Media Platforms
Researchers develop an OASIS-based simulation pipeline utilizing 13,835 large language model agents to evaluate social media interventions, finding that upranking verified news significantly increases engagement across various demographic groups.
Lucen Li, Erin Wertz and others · cs.SI, cs.CL · cross-listed  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
3
Activation-Guided Graph Retrieval for Cognitive Memory Reconstruction in Language Model Systems
The authors propose an activation-guided graph retrieval architecture for cognitive memory reconstruction in language model systems, specifying operational mechanisms and reporting that the central claim is falsifiable if a simpler reranker matches full traversal performance.
Marvin Danig (Achiral Research) · cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.IR  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
4
HiCAP: Hierarchical Cultural Adaptation via Personality for Dialogue Generation
Researchers introduce HiCAP, a hierarchical framework integrating personality traits into culturally adaptive dialogue generation, which demonstrates strong overall performance and effective cultural adaptability across evaluation metrics on CulturalPersonas and CPED.
Lingzhi Shen, Xiaohao Cai and others · cs.CL, cs.LG  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
5
AI Isn't Getting Dumber, We're Getting Pickier: Rising Standards in Human Evaluation of Large Language Models
The authors analyze 1,260,967 blind votes on Chatbot Arena from May 2023 to August 2024, finding that while model rankings remain stable, the share of 'both are bad' votes increases over time due to rising human evaluation standards.
Zihan Chen, Lei Nico Zheng and others · cs.HC, cs.AI, cs.CL · cross-listed  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
 
ARXIV CS.LG
Machine Learning

Recent research addresses energy efficiency in machine learning infrastructure through novel attribution and serverless scaling techniques. The studies introduce methods to fairly allocate energy consumption in large language model batches and extend scale-to-zero capabilities to physical nodes in clusters.

1
Scaling Shapley Energy Attribution to Large LLM Batches
The authors propose StepSplit, a method using a closed-form Shapley value to efficiently attribute energy consumption to individual requests in large language model batches based on shared and request-specific inference step energies.
Qi Luo, Ziwen Wang and others · cs.DC, cs.AR, cs.PF  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
2
Energy-Proportional Serverless Computing on SBC Clusters
The authors present a platform built on tinyFaaS for single-board computer clusters that extends scale-to-zero to physical nodes, reducing mean power draw by 39.6 percent on a bursty workload and 46.7 percent on a sparse one.
Yuliya Sasinskaya, Natalie Carl and others · cs.DC  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
 
ARXIV EESS.SY
Systems and Control

Recent research addresses battery management systems through robust state-parameter estimation in second-order Thévenin models. The study introduces a dual identification and online estimation scheme that achieves exact parameter recovery and exponential convergence of states and capacity under verifiable conditions.

1
Robust State-Parameter Estimation in Second-Order Th\'evenin Batteries
The authors propose a scheme for estimating the full states and parameters of a second-order Thévenin battery model using measured current features, finding that the method achieves exact identification, rapid series resistance recovery, and exponentially convergent online estimates under verifiable excitation conditions.
Wissam Khennoune, G. Q. Bao Tran and others · eess.SY, cs.SY, math.OC  ·  Abstract  ·  PDF  ·  Discuss
